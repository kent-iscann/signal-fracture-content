# South Africa

> Africa's most industrialized economy; China's largest African trading partner ($53.7B in 2025); pivotal geostrategic position at Cape of Good Hope; faces deepening structural dependence dilemma.

## Key Facts

- **Capital:** Pretoria (executive), Cape Town (legislative), Bloemfontein (judicial)
- **Key ports:** Durban (continent's busiest container gateway), Richards Bay (bulk export hub), Cape Town
- **Strategic position:** Cape of Good Hope sea route — vital alternative to Suez Canal
- **Bilateral trade (2025):** $53.7B total ($13B exports to China, $23B imports from China)
- **Critical minerals:** Chrome (80% of world reserves), platinum group metals, manganese, iron ore, coal, gold
- **Key Chinese actors:** PowerChina, Jiangxi Copper, various SOEs
- **Key agreements:** CADEPA Framework Agreement on Economic Partnership for Shared Prosperity (May 2026), zero-tariff regime

## Trade Imbalance

South Africa is China's largest trading partner in Africa, but the relationship is structurally unequal. In 2025:
- SA exported ~$13B to China, >2/3 of which was unprocessed ores and metals
- SA imported ~$23B in Chinese machinery, electronics, batteries, and telecom equipment
- Bilateral trade of $53.7B

Through July 2026, two-way trade reached **$37.8B** ($16B Chinese exports, $21B imports). Gold accounts for 35.6% of SA exports to China (single largest component), PGMs 19%, diamonds 3.2%, chrome ore 20%, manganese ore 8.8%, iron ore 14%. SA is Africa's most diversified mining economy with trade spread across four distinct mineral categories (China-Global South Project).

## CADEPA Framework Agreement

The Framework Agreement on Economic Partnership for Shared Prosperity (CADEPA), effective May 1, 2026, allows eligible South African goods to enter China duty-free. South Africa secured this without yielding reciprocal tariff concessions (protecting its automotive sector).
- Same zero-tariff terms offered to all 53 African countries with diplomatic ties to China
- Critics argue duty-free access alone does not address the structural trade imbalance without accompanying investments in local processing and technology transfer

## Strategic Dilemma

As articulated by FPRI analyst Raghvendra Kumar (July 2026), South Africa faces a deepening strategic dilemma:

### China's Embedding
- Chinese firms operate or hold stakes in 78 of Africa's 231 commercial ports across 32 countries
- >30 African countries now use China's BeiDou Navigation Satellite System for maritime traffic management — reducing dependence on US GPS
- China exports its "port-park-city" model linking ports with industrial parks, manufacturing zones, and urban development
- Chinese state-owned enterprises supply port operating systems, AI logistics platforms, customs digitization, and cybersecurity architecture

### Naval Dimension
- In January 2026, South Africa hosted the 'Will for Peace' multinational naval exercise off Cape Town involving China, Russia, Iran, and UAE
- PLAN vessels have made repeated port calls to Durban and Richards Bay
- China's latest Five-Year Plan calls for transition from "participation" to "leadership" in global ocean governance

### Policy Response
- FPRI recommends: maintain sovereign ownership of critical infrastructure; enforce binding local value-addition commitments; safeguard maritime domain awareness as national security priority; pursue "equity in development" partnership rather than disengagement

## Key Developments
- **May 2026:** South Africa opens freight rail network to private train operators — biggest rail reform in decades, affecting 20,000+ km network
- **June 2026:** EU launches first-ever investment roadshow in SA, announcing 12B package under the EU-South Africa Clean Trade and Investment Partnership
- **June 22, 2026:** Deputy President Paul Mashatile speaks at 4th China International Supply Chain Expo in Beijing, stating SA wants to be "more than a mine" — aims to build manufacturing units, process minerals, and serve as Beijing's gateway to wider African market
- **August 2-6, 2026:** SA Electricity and Energy Minister Kgosientsho Ramokgopa leads high-level delegation to Beijing including Trade Minister Parks Tau and Deputy IR Minister Thandi Moraka, pitching R2.2 trillion ($122B) energy investment programme under Integrated Resource Plan 2025. Package targets 105 GW new generation capacity and 14,500 km of new transmission lines. China Development Bank executive VP pledges support. Delegation includes Eskom executives, provincial premiers, and DFIs. The stated goal is to rebuild SA's industrial base through local manufacturing of transformers, battery storage, and solar PV equipment. Geopolitical backdrop: US aid cuts (Feb 2025) and tariffs narrow SA's Western alternatives.
- **Through July 2026:** SA-China two-way trade reached $37.8B ($16B Chinese exports, $21B imports). Gold accounts for 35.6% of SA exports to China (single largest component), PGMs 19%, diamonds 3.2%, chrome ore 20%, manganese ore 8.8%, iron ore 14% (China-Global South Project).
- **September 8, 2026:** Three Chinese OEMs confirm building transformer, wire and pylon manufacturing facilities in South Africa — concrete outcomes from the Ramokgopa Beijing energy delegation. SA leveraging SEZ incentives and local manufacturing demands to attract Chinese industrial investment in renewable energy transmission infrastructure (Pinsent Masons).
- **September 2026:** South Africa and China discuss establishing a China-Africa Innovation Alliance on Mining and Metallurgy to advance mineral processing research, battery materials development, and technology transfer. South African researchers developing battery-grade manganese sulphate and NMC precursors. Feasibility study for 10,000-litre NMC precursor plant completed (Green Building Africa).
- **September 16, 2026:** Team Europe (EU, Germany, Denmark, Netherlands) launches two Global Gateway projects worth >R1 billion (EUR57.8M) for South Africa's green hydrogen and minerals/battery value chains. Implemented by GIZ and KfW. Part of broader R200B+ Team Europe Investment Package (Green Building Africa).
