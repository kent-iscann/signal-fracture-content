# Rodrigo Paz

President of Bolivia since November 2025. Son of former president Jaime Paz Zamora. Campaigned as centrist but governed with right-wing coalition after entering office. Key decisions: eliminated fuel subsidies (triggering inflation), struck deals with right-wing parties in Congress, excluded populist VP from cabinet. Facing existential crisis after 50+ days of protests demanding resignation. Declared state of emergency June 20, 2026.
