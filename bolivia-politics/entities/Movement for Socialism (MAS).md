# Movement for Socialism (MAS)

Bolivia's long-dominant socialist party led by Evo Morales. Governed nearly uninterrupted from 2006-2025. Ousted in 2025 election. Now in opposition but retains significant support among Indigenous and rural working-class constituencies.

Party collapse driven by three-way infighting between Evo Morales, his successor Luis Arce, and Andrónico Rodríguez (young cocalero leader who might have inherited the MAS mantle). This infighting effectively destroyed the party in the months leading up to the August 2025 election.

MAS's fall also reflects a broader crisis of legitimacy: exhaustion of the neo-statist, extractivist economic model dependent on hydrocarbon revenues, widespread corruption, and the party's corporate system of mediation between state and social movements that limited political opposition.
