# Armenia-Azerbaijan Peace Process: Structural Notes

## The Sequential Condition Chain (as of May 2026)

The peace process between Armenia and Azerbaijan operates on an explicit sequential condition model, publicly outlined by Azerbaijani Ambassador to Turkey Rashad Mammadov (May 19, 2026):

```
Constitutional amendment (remove territorial claims)
    → Peace agreement signing (initialed Aug 2025, not yet formally signed)
        → Border openings (Turkey-Armenia AND Armenia-Azerbaijan simultaneously)
```

**Key implications:**
- Azerbaijan's primary blocker is Armenia's constitution, not the peace treaty text itself
- Turkey explicitly links its border opening to the Armenia-Azerbaijan peace deal (not independent of it)
- The sequence means even if Armenia amends its constitution, the peace treaty must be signed before borders open
- Pashinyan has pledged constitutional changes, but a referendum is expected only AFTER the June 2026 elections

## Domestic Constraints (Armenia)

- Pashinyan's Civil Contract won 49.81% of parliamentary seats (June 7, 2026 election) — majority but short of the 2/3 supermajority needed for constitutional reform
- Opposition: Strong Armenia (Samvel Karapetyan) 23.29%, Armenia alliance (Kocharyan) 9.9%, Prosperous Armenia 4%. Total opposition + pro-Russian bloc ~37%
- Karapetyan is under house arrest in Russia; called elections "shameful." Turnout: 58%
- Pashinyan's narrower mandate makes constitutional referendum harder but Carnegie analysts argue this makes a visible "peace dividend" more urgent, not a reason to delay
- Constitutional referendum expected later in 2026 or 2027; Pashinyan has framed it as the start of Armenia's "Fourth Republic"

## Aliyev's Escalating Preconditions (June 2026)

Post-election, Aliyev has added several new demands beyond constitutional change:
- **Resettlement of 300,000 Azerbaijanis** in Armenia under the "Western Azerbaijan" campaign
- **Return of former Azerbaijani enclaves** within Armenia
- **Territorial adjustments** via border "delimitation and demarcation"
- Azerbaijan formed the "Community of Western Azerbaijan" organization and circulated appeals to all UN member states (January and March 2023)
- A "Western Azerbaijan" conference was held in the US Congress halls on June 24, 2026
- Analysts assess Aliyev is in no rush to sign a peace deal, pressing for additional concessions as Pashinyan accepts existing demands

## Border Reopening Target

- Deputy Foreign Minister Vahan Kostanyan announced in Brussels (June 24, 2026) that Armenia has set **2030** as the target year for full border reopening with Turkey and Azerbaijan
- Certain transport routes could operationalize earlier as regional projects advance

## Key Railway/Transport Developments

- **Akhalkalaki-Kars railway (Georgia-Turkey):** Opened for Armenian trade May 24, 2026. Built as part of Baku-Tbilisi-Kars project. 105 km total (76 km Turkey, 29 km Georgia).
- **Kars-Gyumri railway (direct Turkey-Armenia):** Idle since 1993. Repair work announced by Pashinyan. This is the ONLY rail link directly between Turkey and Armenia.
- **Yeraskh junction:** Under Russian concession management. Pashinyan announced Armenia will repair it anyway.
- **Azerbaijan rail transit to Armenia:** Opened October 2025. By May 2026: 100K tons of grain, 8.5K tons diesel, ~4K tons gasoline transited.
- **TRIPP corridor:** US-Armenia agreement signed May 2026. DFC implementation structure: TDC US (wholly DFC-owned Delaware corp) and TDC (bi-national JV in Armenia, 74% DFC / 26% Armenia). Multimodal scope includes rail, road, oil/gas pipeline, electricity, fiberoptic. World Bank projections: ~$100M/year transport savings, 0.5-1.0% GDP growth, ~3% export growth, thousands of jobs. No DFC funding committed yet — sub-project equity capped at 40%. DFC creation does not obligate participation in sub-projects.
- **NATO summit diplomatic window:** July 2026 NATO summit in Ankara. Carnegie proposes Erdoğan convene Trump-Pashinyan-Aliyev on the sidelines. Recommended first concrete step: opening Turkey-Armenia border for direct trade and third-country nationals.

- Equipment from 7 countries (up from near-zero before 2022)
- Defense industry: 170B drams invested since 2022
- Fortified border zones constructed along Armenian borders

## Key Recent Developments (Jul 2026)

- **Pashinyan-Mishustin Yekaterinburg meeting (Jul 6):** First post-election visit to Russia. Mishustin extended olive branch but warned on Russian investor protection. Pashinyan reaffirmed EAEU commitment but said mechanisms must "function as intended." EU had disbursed €52M since election and liberalised export rules. Russia = 35% of Armenia's foreign trade; heavy energy dependence.
- **Armenia begins independent railway restoration (Jul 2026):** Pashinyan announced Armenia will repair Gyumri-Akhurik and Yeraskh sections "ourselves" — bypassing Russia's South Caucasus Railway concession (Russian Railways subsidiary, 30-yr concession since 2008, freight traffic halved). Russia has not signalled readiness but won't give up concession. Pashinyan: "Armenia owns the railways."
- **Von der Leyen Yerevan visit (Jul 2-3):** Described Russian pressure as "bordering on economic violence." Announced autonomous trade measures liberalizing ~80% of Armenian exports to EU (~99% agricultural, ~90% beverages). Final €18M of €52M package. Visa liberalization dialogue — target visa-free travel by 2029. Connectivity for Peace package up to €200M, expected to mobilize up to €2B.
- **Grigoryan-Hajiyev Dilijan meeting (Jun 14):** First bilateral Armenia-Azerbaijan meeting at this level on Armenian soil. Purpose: "managing threat of war" from opposition rhetoric on Karabakh. Next meeting in Azerbaijan. Pashinyan's party won election majority same day.
- **TRIPP active phase (Jun 29):** Pashinyan confirmed TRIPP in "active working phase" with no political/financial obstacles. Construction possible before year-end if no technical difficulties. Key US bilateral agreement near ratification.
- **AEI critique of Section 907 waiver:** Michael Rubin argues Trump's waiver allowing US weapons sales to Azerbaijan contradicts peace process. Notes Azerbaijan occupies ~100 sq mi of undisputed Armenian land and maintains dual policy of trading with Iran and helping Russia evade sanctions.

## Key Recent Developments (Aug 2026)

- **Galuzin's December deadline (Aug 11):** Russian DPM Mikhail Galuzin stated Armenia must decide by December whether to hold a referendum on EU vs EAEU membership, warning that EAEU leaders would otherwise take "appropriate joint steps." Pashinyan rejected the timetable: on Aug 7 said a referendum is impossible now as there is "no subject," on Aug 13 said a vote would happen once Armenia formally applies for EU membership.
- **Constitutional draft finalized (Aug 2026):** Armenia's Justice Ministry confirmed the constitutional draft has been finalized and is under review within the ruling party but has not been published — constraining informed public debate. Pashinyan has not ruled out a repeat referendum if the first one fails.
- **Armenia awards $215M to Iranian firms for Syunik infrastructure (Aug 2026):** Armenia reportedly awarded ~$215M in contracts to Iranian construction firms for the North-South Highway in Syunik province — the same province where the TRIPP corridor will operate. Iranian VP attended Kajaran tunnel inauguration in June. Pashinyan attended Khamenei's funeral to signal limits of westward tilt.
- **CES Intelligence scenario analysis (Aug 2026):** Comprehensive geopolitical risk assessment frames Azerbaijan's "transit trap." Three scenarios: (A) Route Stable 40-45% — TRIPP operational 2027, peace treaty signed H1 2027; (B) Route Contested 35-40% — periodic disruptions from Iranian grey-zone actions; (C) Route Contested & Conflagration 15-20% — Iran escalates to sustained military operations.

- **Aliyev offers electricity supply to Armenia (Aug 11, reported Aug 13):** Aliyev stated Azerbaijan could supply Armenia with electricity, citing Metsamor NPP dependency. Azerbaijani specialists conducted surveys in Armenia, identified power line pylon sites. Power line through Armenia to Nakhchivan ready, estimated 6 months to complete. Pashinyan confirmed AI infrastructure demands may require additional electricity sources.

- **CEPA analysis: TRIPP first anniversary (Aug 27):** Emil Avdaliani analysis reports US companies reached ~$8B in deals with Azerbaijan under TRIPP framework. Azerbaijani railway link to Armenia expected complete 2027. Construction on Armenian soil to begin late 2026-early 2027. 60,000 tons of goods transited Armenia-Azerbaijan border in past year.
- **Arab News: Fidan raises joint defense framework (Aug 28):** Turkish FM Hakan Fidan raised possibility of joint defense framework involving South Caucasus countries as peace process advances.
- **DW profile: Konstantin Sokolov — Russian-American head of TRIPP+ (Aug 29):** Detailed profile of Sokolov, appointed to head the TRIPP+ Enterprise Fund (registered as Trans-Caspian Enterprise Fund, $200M+ capital, operating through 2036). Key revelations: Sokolov (52) emigrated from Russia to US at 21, founded IJS Investments in Chicago. Before his official appointment, he had already acquired Viva Armenia (largest telecom, 2024), Rostelecom's Armenian subsidiary, and the Teghut copper-molybdenum mine (~450M tons ore, Armenia's 2nd largest reserves) from Russia's sanctioned VTB Bank via a third-party company. He is a major Republican donor (~$11M to MAGA over one year). He met Pashinyan and Mirzoyan in early August accompanied by Aryeh Lightstone (senior adviser to US special envoy Steve Witkoff). Analysis: APRI's Poghosyan identifies two reasons for Sokolov's appointment — political (subtle gesture to ease Moscow's criticism) and pragmatic (deep understanding of post-Soviet business). Social media speculation about Sokolov as a "compromise figure" between Moscow and Washington is rebutted by his public criticism of the Russian government and war in Ukraine. The scale of US investment ($200M+ through 2036) flags Washington's long-term strategic intentions in the region. Lightstone told CivilNet the initial funding could be a "starting point."
