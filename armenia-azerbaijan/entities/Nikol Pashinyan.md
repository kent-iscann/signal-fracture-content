# Nikol Pashinyan

**Role:** Prime Minister of Armenia (since 2018)
**Party:** Civil Contract

## Profile
Pashinyan came to power in 2018 through the Velvet Revolution, a peaceful protest movement that ousted the long-ruling Republican Party. A former journalist and opposition figure, he positioned himself as a reformer committed to anti-corruption, democratic governance, and a foreign policy pivot toward the West.

## Key Positions
- **Peace with Azerbaijan:** Has consistently signaled willingness to recognize Azerbaijani sovereignty over Nagorno-Karabakh in exchange for security guarantees and a comprehensive peace treaty. This stance is deeply unpopular among many Armenians who view it as capitulation.
- **Western Pivot:** Has systematically distanced Armenia from Russia, hosting EU and Ukrainian leaders, signing strategic partnerships with the US, and pursuing normalization with Turkey.
- **Domocratic Reform:** Has pledged to reform Armenia's institutions, including controversial plans to reform the Armenian Apostolic Church's governance.

## Political Situation
Following the June 2026 parliamentary elections, Pashinyan's Civil Contract party won 49.8% of votes — enough for a parliamentary majority. The election was marked by documented Russian interference aimed at bolstering pro-Russian opposition parties. Pashinyan overcame a pressure campaign by Moscow and won a mandate to continue peace talks with Azerbaijan and the Western pivot.

## Key Relationships
- **Ilham Aliyev (Azerbaijan):** Negotiating partner for peace treaty. Relations remain tense but both sides have committed to a diplomatic process. Azerbaijan has begun supplying Armenia with oil/fuel and enabling wheat transit from Kazakhstan/Russia through its territory.
- **Vladimir Putin (Russia):** Relations have deteriorated sharply. Putin has publicly warned Pashinyan against the Western pivot, comparing it to Ukraine's path before 2014. Russia's concession regime on Armenian railways continues until 2038, limiting Yerevan's independent railway strategy.
- **Recep Tayyip Erdoğan (Turkey):** Normalization process ongoing. Trade resumed in May 2026 after 33 years. The Akhalkalaki-Kars railway opened for Armenian trade on May 24, 2026. Border remains closed but railway links have reopened. Turkish VP Cevdet Yılmaz made highest-level visit to Armenia since 2008.
- **Donald Trump (US):** Trump brokered the August 2025 peace framework. The TRUMP Route for International Peace and Prosperity (TRIPP) corridor is a key US-backed initiative. A final TRIPP agreement is expected to be signed.

## Military Reforms
Following the Republic Day 2026 parade, Pashinyan announced a fundamental shift in Armenian military doctrine: the army's sole mission is defense of internationally recognized sovereign territory, with no operations outside borders except international peacekeeping missions. This shift enabled access to arms from seven countries. Armenia's defense industry received 170 billion drams in government investment since 2022. New fortified defense zones have been constructed along Armenia's borders.
