# Moro Islamic Liberation Front (MILF)

The largest and most politically sophisticated Moro insurgent group in the Philippines. Founded in 1984 as a splinter from the MNLF, the MILF sought an independent Islamic state in Mindanao before negotiating toward autonomy.

## Current status
- Signed the Comprehensive Agreement on the Bangsamoro (CAB) with the Philippine government in 2014
- Transitioned from armed movement to governing authority within BARMM
- First parliamentary elections scheduled for September 14, 2026 — a defining moment for the peace process
- Intra-MILF rivalries over electoral strategy, resource control, and relationships with Manila are intensifying
- In May 2026, forged a joint anti-ISIS front with the Philippine Army in Lanao del Norte
- **May 2026:** Mohagher Iqbal removed as MP and education minister over corruption allegations; ~37,000 MILF supporters held emergency peace rally in Cotabato City demanding reinstatement of Murad Ebrahim and full CAB implementation.
- **May 2026:** Bangsamoro Federalist Party (BFP) formed by Macacua supporters — a direct challenge to the MILF's dominant United Bangsamoro Justice Party (UBJP), signaling a major internal split.
- **2026:** CCAA data shows clan feuding (rido) at 43 incidents in first four months (vs. 48 for all of 2025); several firefights involved MILF commands, exposing flaws in the decommissioning process as weapons remain in MILF hands.
- **June 2026:** MILF Central Committee suspends Abdulraof Macacua as BIAF Chief of Staff — retaliatory move over BFP formation and Iqbal ouster. Rift penetrates MILF's armed wing.
- **June 2026:** 1,000+ MILF members (including Cotabato City Mayor Bruce Matabalao) leave UBJP for BFP. Breakaway party now institutionalized as MILF's primary electoral rival.
- **June 2026:** COA finds P2.2–4.9 billion in irregularities at MBHTE under Iqbal's tenure. Macacua orders charges before Ombudsman.
- **2026 (RSIS CTTA):** UBJP only represents Maguindanao, not the wider indigenous population. Census data shows locals trust Manila government more than Bangsamoro authorities, highlighting precarious state of the UBJP.
- **2026 (RSIS CTTA):** MILF decommissioning suspended — OPAPRU failed to deliver socioeconomic programmes for combatant reintegration, leading to BTA's inability to support MILF combatants. Suspension is a potential flashpoint requiring monitoring.
- **June 26 & May 23:** MILF 123rd Base Command (Tagoloan) and 127th Base Command (Munai) declare DI-MG and NPA persona non grata in Lanao del Norte — expands MILF-Army counter-terror cooperation.
- **June 7 (MindaNews):** UBJP has no women in its top 10 party nominees for the September 14 parliamentary election. Only one woman is a #1 nominee among 13 parties (Mahardika/MNLF).
- **July 15:** Marcos appoints Mel Sarmiento as chair of Gov't Peace Implementing Panel — fills long-vacant counterpart post to MILF's Iqbal. Key MILF demand for resuming decommissioning.
- **July 18:** Maguindanao del Sur Rep. Esmael Mangudadatu defects from UBJP to BFP — adds to exodus of local officials joining breakaway party.
- **July 2026:** MILF's Commander Bravo publicly warns MILF prepared to return to armed struggle if CAB commitments remain unfulfilled — most explicit reversion threat since 2014 deal.
- **July 2026:** COMELEC Resolution 11248 designates UBJP as dominant majority party and BFP as dominant minority party — formalizes MILF political schism as electoral structure.
- **July 20:** Multisectoral Peace Coalition dialogue with Sarmiento; Sarmiento commits to MILF coordination over peace agreement implementation.
- **July 22:** Marcos distributes P3.4B to BARMM but does not meet MILF leadership or address CAB implementation.
- **July 27:** SONA 2026 devotes 40 seconds to peace process — no CAB, decommissioning, or normalization mentions. MILF grievances unaddressed.

## Significance
The MILF's transformation from insurgent group to political authority is the central variable in the Mindanao peace process. If the September 2026 elections proceed credibly, the track record of Islamist governance in BARMM will influence whether splinter groups retain recruitment appeal. If elections are marred by fraud, violence, or Manila interference, spoiler factions could rearm. The emergence of the BFP as a breakaway faction and the Iqbal corruption crisis represent the most serious internal challenge to MILF cohesion since the 2014 CAB.

## Key figures
- Murad Ebrahim — MILF Chairperson and BARMM Interim Chief Minister
- Mohagher Iqbal — Former MP/education minister, removed May 2026 over corruption allegations
- Abdulraof Macacua — Interim Minister, political patron of the breakaway BFP
- Mel Senen Sarmiento — Presidential Adviser on Peace, Reconciliation and Unity (appointed 2026)
- **August 2026:** CCAA data shows MILF internal divisions (UBJP vs BFP) fueling violence — 8 BIAF base commanders replaced after pledging to BFP. Nabalawag-Kadayangan SGA violence involving MILF commanders displaces ~1,500 households. Stalled decommissioning means weapons remain in MILF members' hands during election season — CCAA warns of risk.
